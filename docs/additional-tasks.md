# Additional repeatable tasks

These occasional tasks use the same candidate evidence and criteria as the ten skills. They do not need separate skill wrappers until repeated use justifies them.

## Positioning and public-profile audit

Start with the supplied master, evidence register, desired roles, and approved public-use facts. Identify the career through-line and strongest supported outcomes. Check titles, dates, and claims across the resume and user-supplied public profiles. Use current public research only when authorized and necessary; distinguish identity matches from namesakes. Save proposed changes with exact source and approval state. Public edits require authorization. [The fictional positioning example](../examples/profile/positioning.md) connects evidence, a resume headline, and a proposed profile summary.

## Compensation comparison

Use the candidate's own minimum, preferred range, timing needs, and liquidity assumptions. Separate recurring salary, dependable bonus, salable public equity, one-time signing cash, and illiquid private equity. Show assumptions for bonus payout, vesting, sale restrictions, volatility, taxes when requested, and timing; do not infer a package from a posting's base range alone. An attractive private valuation is not spendable pay. [The fictional comparison](../examples/strategy/compensation.md) demonstrates a simple screen. Specific financial, tax, and legal decisions require current authoritative evidence and appropriate expertise; the starter supplies no employer-specific financial model.

## Consulting or fractional validation

Write a concrete buyer problem, reachable buyer profile, deliverable, duration, fee hypothesis, and evidence needed to test willingness to pay. Use interviews and proposed experiments to refine the offer; keep expressions of interest separate from signed work or income. Check time commitments and conflicts against the user's situation before treating this as a bridge. [The fictional experiment](../examples/strategy/consulting.md) shows a scoped offer and unresolved commercial assumptions. Drafting does not authorize contacting prospects or committing terms.
