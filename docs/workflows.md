Try these prompts:

- “Use $job-role-research to assess this posting, verify its official availability, and save a brief against my criteria.”
- “Use $job-lamp-networking for these new or partial companies; preserve my decisions and current paths.”
- “Use $job-conversation-prep for my 30-minute informational conversation; give me a quick review first.”
- “Use $job-application-package with this resume source and these requested answers; save a reviewable manifest.”
- “Use $job-interview-story with my notes for this episode; identify the facts still needed.”
- “Use $job-conversation-capture with my completed-meeting notes and show supported changes and proposed follow-ups.”
- “Use $job-search-review and give me the most useful next actions across existing pursuits.”

For an example walkthrough say explicitly: “Using only the fictional examples, show how the preparation follows from the evidence and role record. Do not create a real candidate record or contact anyone.”

Use [templates](../templates/README.md) for new records, [the data model](data-model.md) for authority and state, and [additional tasks](additional-tasks.md) for positioning, compensation, and consulting. Activity maintenance and integrations are optional documented procedures. No skill invocation creates an automation.
