# LAMP Posting

Read the company list and Posting checkpoints; preserve networking paths and candidate Advocacy/Motivation choices. Research selected untracked, stale, or partial company boards using their official current employer sources.

Use this convention: 3 means a relevant live official posting, 2 means other current openings without a target role confirmed, and 1 means a verified empty current official board. Failed access or a search-engine miss is unresolved, not 1. State the candidate's relevance criteria, board identity, actual observation date, method, and limitations.

Keep role links and dated evidence in research notes; CSV cells can retain plain-text URLs where useful, but the scalar Posting field stays the score. Do not conflate a company score with role availability or candidate interest. Retain closed history and existing decisions; use role research for a material individual opening.

Update the selected Posting checkpoints only after evidence is saved. Research can remain partial after blocked verification. Run `python3 scripts/job_search.py check` and report verified changes and blockers. This workflow does not change role stages, create CRM writes, or contact anyone. The [fictional posting notes](../../examples/research/postings.md) are formatting examples, not live evidence.
