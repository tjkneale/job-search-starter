# Company diligence

Read the candidate's criteria and the company's existing history. Investigate only the depth needed for the pursuit decision: product and buyers; ownership/funding and financial durability; leadership and transitions; growth evidence; competition; values/end-use concerns; and the role's mandate, authority, compensation, and access.

Use company materials, official postings, filings, regulators, and other primary evidence for material facts. Label independent estimates, anonymous employment reviews, and marketing claims. Funding, headcount, customer counts, and pricing do not establish revenue or audited health. Record actual source dates and observation dates.

Write a brief conclusion, material fit/risk findings, and questions for the employer. Separate observed facts, interpretation, and unknowns. Preserve earlier research when refreshing and label superseded observations. Use [the company template](../../templates/company-brief.md) under the personal workspace.

The candidate owns Motivation scores and interest decisions; an assistant ranking can be offered separately with its criteria. Do not overwrite a score from enthusiasm or inferred approval. The [fictional company brief](../../examples/opportunities/companies/cedarline.md) shows the evidence boundary. Diligence does not authorize outreach, applications, or external publication.
