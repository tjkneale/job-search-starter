# LAMP networking

Read the candidate's context, company list, existing paths, and `research/company-status.json`. Identify new or partial work from checkpoints, not empty cells. The starter uses CSV; spreadsheet adaptations must preserve native links, formulas, and formatting. Use column headers and stable normalized company identities rather than row numbers.

For selected companies, verify identity, then inspect direct relationships, named mutuals or intermediaries, shared affiliations, and credible former-employee/community paths proportionately. Signed-in observations establish connection degree and mutuals; public searches do not. Verify relevant roles and alumni facts rather than inferring graduation or endorsement from attendance or a group badge.

Record actual observation date, sources, target, specific intermediary/affinity, observed or candidate-reported status, and limitations in private dated evidence. A second-degree badge without a named intermediary is not a complete introduction route. There is no quota of contacts to fill.

Append distinct routes without replacing existing paths, notes, links, row order, or candidate decisions. Advocacy is candidate-controlled; leave it blank for new companies. Preserve Motivation and Posting. Explicitly label former employees and reported promises.

Update only the selected networking checkpoints, leaving unfinished work `partial` and recording completed no-path research as `no_paths_found`. Check structural consistency with `python3 scripts/job_search.py check`. Report useful paths, research boundaries, and pending work. No research step sends a request or establishes willingness to refer. See [the fictional research](../../examples/research/networking.md).
