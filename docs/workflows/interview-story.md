# Interview stories

Read the candidate's evidence register, relevant episode notes, approved public-use claims, and existing story bank. Reuse the episode's stable ID. Do not import invented example accomplishments into the user's story.

Separate situation, actual authority, personal decisions/actions, team work, and result. Check every number's source and definition; distinguish company context from candidate causation. Keep uncertain facts as questions, and private corroboration separate from externally usable wording. Candidate-approved wording remains authoritative unless new evidence creates a material conflict.

Use [the story template](../../templates/interview-story.md) to write a defensible one-line truth, short and longer speaking versions, judgment/learning, and relevant follow-ups. Save in `workspace/interview/stories/` and link it in the bank with question mapping, maturity, and external-use status.

`Discovery` and `Draft` describe development. Use `Interview-ready` only when evidence, disclosure, and actual candidate rehearsal are supported; a written polished answer alone is insufficient. Preserve useful previous versions and corrections with provenance. See [the fictional draft story](../../examples/interview/stories/pilot.md). Story writing does not establish a completed candidate activity.
