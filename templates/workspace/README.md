# Personal workspace

This blank skeleton is copied into gitignored `workspace/` by `python3 scripts/job_search.py init`. Add your own facts and private evidence. Nothing from `examples/` is copied into your profile.

Fill `profile/candidate.md`, `profile/evidence.md`, and `strategy/criteria.md`; supply your current resume. Create records in the named folders as you use the workflows. The public starter's documentation describes their purposes. No real records should be moved into the public example folder.
