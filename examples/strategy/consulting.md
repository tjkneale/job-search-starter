# Invented consulting validation experiment

> Fictional example. All people, organizations, events, claims, decisions, and evidence references below are invented; nothing is a live posting or genuine activity evidence.


Buyer hypothesis: small workflow-software teams uncertain which customer friction to prioritize. Proposed offer: a two-week discovery-and-pilot design sprint producing a ranked friction map and measurement plan. Fee hypothesis: $6,000 fixed; no buyer has agreed.

Test: conduct a few authorized buyer conversations to learn whether the problem is urgent, funded, and owned. Ask about current workarounds, cost of delay, decision process, and a useful deliverable. No prospect contact is authorized in this snapshot.

Evidence states: offer draft exists; buyer interest unknown; contract absent; revenue zero. Before treating it as a paycheck bridge, assess actual signed timing, payment reliability, delivery effort, search time, and conflicts. A fee hypothesis is not income.
