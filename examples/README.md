# Fictional walkthrough

All data in this folder is invented. Riley Morgan, the employers, contacts, compensation, claims, sources, and events are fictional. Links ending in `.example` are reserved illustrative URLs. The scenario is frozen at April 15, 2025; it makes no statement about today's openings or any real candidate. Scenario dates illustrate date discipline and are not actual research dates or documentary evidence.

## Follow the search

1. [Candidate context](profile/candidate.md), [evidence](profile/evidence.md), and [criteria](strategy/criteria.md) establish what can be said and what matters.
2. [Cedarline's role](opportunities/roles/cedarline-product.md) maps requirements to evidence. [Harborlight's closed role](opportunities/roles/harborlight-platform.md) retains history using `Won't apply`, since no application was submitted.
3. [The company list](opportunities/company-list.csv), [networking evidence](research/networking.md), [posting notes](research/postings.md), and [separate checkpoints](research/company-status.json) distinguish candidate scores from research completion.
4. [Company diligence](opportunities/companies/cedarline.md) and [a short email draft](networking/request.md) make access and learning concrete. The draft is unsent.
5. [Conversation prep](interview/cedarline-prep.md) uses [a reusable story](interview/stories/pilot.md). [Captured notes](opportunities/conversations/cedarline.md) show how a fictional completed conversation changes assumptions without implying an application.
6. [The package](resumes/applications/cedarline/package.md) names its source and outputs. Its tailored resume and answers are ready for review; nothing was submitted.
7. [The review](research/search-review.md) prioritizes next actions. [The activity log](profile/activity-log.md) keeps completed and pending actions separate, without asserting benefit eligibility.
8. [Positioning](profile/positioning.md), [compensation](strategy/compensation.md), and [consulting](strategy/consulting.md) demonstrate occasional adjacent tasks.

Run `python3 scripts/job_search.py demo` to inspect the role and company state, or `python3 scripts/job_search.py crm-preview --root examples` for allowlisted local metadata. These commands read files, not websites or accounts. Use `init` to create a blank personal workspace; example facts are never copied into it.
